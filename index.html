Vision

Agents learned by doing. Robots are next.

Human demos don’t scale. RL environments (interaction + reward) do.

Every robot demo needs a person, a robot and hours of teleoperation, so data grows with headcount. Interaction plus reward grows with compute: once the environment exists, a model can run millions of attempts and learn from each one.

The problem

Robot models are general. Every deployment is specific.

Every real deployment needs adapting to a specific task, robot, set of objects and site. Today there are three ways to do that, and each one is expensive.

  1. 01

    Real-world data is slow and costly

  2. 02

    Physical trials are hard to repeat

  3. 03

    Simulations are built by hand

The gap between general intelligence and reliable deployment is where we sit.

An environment a team can post-train and evaluate on, not just a visual copy of the scene.

The harness is our core. It calibrates the simulation against the real video so it behaves like the real scene, and validates that tasks, rewards and resets work before a customer trains on it.

Built on existing simulators such as MuJoCo and Isaac Lab. Our value is automating the engineering around them.
